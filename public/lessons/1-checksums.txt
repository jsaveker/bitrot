Spot the invisible change
=========================

A cryptographic hash turns a file into a fixed-size fingerprint. SHA-256 produces 256 bits, shown as 64 hexadecimal characters.

Change one bit in the file and its hash will almost certainly change. Compare against a trusted hash of the original to detect damage.

A matching hash is useful evidence of integrity, but a checksum alone does not prove who created a file. A hash also cannot restore missing or damaged data. You need an intact copy for that.
