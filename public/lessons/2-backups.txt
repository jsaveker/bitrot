Bring the signal back
=====================

A checksum can tell you a file changed. It cannot reconstruct the original. An intact backup can.

In this challenge, save a copy, damage the working message, then restore the saved bytes. The matching SHA-256 fingerprints confirm recovery.

This tab-memory demonstration is not a real backup: closing or reloading the tab clears it. Real backups need independent storage and tested restores. The 3-2-1 approach keeps three copies on two kinds of media, with one copy off-site. Bitrot's freeze command does not create a backup; local files never decay automatically.

Open /learn for the interactive challenge.
